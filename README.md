## 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm a **Computer Science student and Backend Developer** with a strong interest in building reliable, maintainable, and production-ready applications.

My main focus is **Backend Development**, especially with **Node.js, NestJS, Express, PostgreSQL, REST APIs, Authentication & Authorization, and API Architecture**. I also have hands-on experience with **ASP.NET Core** and enjoy exploring different backend technologies and architectural approaches.

Beyond writing backend code, I'm deeply interested in what happens **after development** — from **Docker and CI/CD** to **cloud deployment, Linux, infrastructure, monitoring, and production environments**. I'm particularly focused on building systems that are not only functional, but also easier to deploy, observe, maintain, and scale.

I'm currently growing toward a **Cloud & DevOps-oriented Backend career**, while continuously strengthening my foundations in **AWS, DevOps practices, system design, security, and backend architecture**.

I also enjoy experimenting with **AI-powered applications and automation**, especially when AI can be integrated into real backend products rather than treated as a standalone feature.

> **Build the backend. Understand the infrastructure. Ship the product. Keep learning.**

- 🔭 Building production-oriented Backend projects with **NestJS & PostgreSQL**
